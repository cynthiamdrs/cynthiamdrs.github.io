Poster presentation (Poster #1, Session 2) at the [BaYSM - Bayesian Young Statisticians Meeting](https://baysm2026.github.io) on two Monte Carlo simulation studies examining LDA and STM topic model performance under varying corpus conditions. Study I investigated LDA sensitivity in small corpora; Study II compared LDA and STM under temporally evolving topic prevalence, revealing a counterintuitive crossover in model performance favourable to LDA for longer documents.

Joint work with John Quigley and Matthew Revie (University of Strathclyde).
